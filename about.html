About LifeStream

Coordinating community blood donation drives and multi-specialty wellness camps since 2012.

Expert Care Built on Volunteer Action

LifeStream is dedicated to bridging health resources and community needs. We organize mobile blood donation camps, free pediatric checkups, eye screenings, and diagnostic checkups. By partnering with certified medical volunteers and local coordinators, we ensure wellness resources are accessible to all.

Our digital portal enables blood donors and community volunteers to register for drives, view upcoming camp schedules, and download screening reports and blood certifications securely online.

Our History & Legacy

From a small neighborhood initiative to a trusted healthcare campaign, explore the milestones of our journey.

2012

LifeStream Founded

Started as a local network to connect emergency blood donors with regional hospitals.

2016

Mobile Units

Launched specialized blood collection vans to coordinate on-site drives in schools and offices.

2020

Health Camps Launch

Expanded our program to offer comprehensive wellness checkups and free screening camps.

Our Mission

To organize safe, sterile blood donation drives and comprehensive health checkup camps, bringing critical wellness resources directly to communities.

We strive to build a seamless platform for volunteers, donors, and specialists, utilizing digital records to deliver immediate health reports and coordination support. Every camp we establish is driven by our core commitment to public welfare and community care.

Our Vision

To cultivate a robust national network of volunteers and donors, ensuring no patient lacks blood and every neighborhood has access to basic health screening.

We envision a community where preventative health and blood donations are easily accessible, fostering proactive public health campaigns. By scaling our coordinates and coordinating with expert volunteers, we aim to build a healthier tomorrow for everyone.
