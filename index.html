Eligibility Guidelines

Can You Donate Blood?

Age & Weight

Donors must be between 18 and 65 years old and weigh at least 50 kg (110 lbs) to participate in blood donation drives safely.

General Health

Must be in good general health with normal temperature, blood pressure, and pulse rate, and free from active infections or antibiotic courses.

Donation Frequency

Whole blood can be donated every 56 days (8 weeks). Platelet donors can donate every 7 days (up to 24 times per year).
